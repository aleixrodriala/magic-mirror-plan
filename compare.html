3 Better Build Architectures (Replacing the Pale Pine Box)

I. Floating Aluminum Edge + Shadow-Box

Shown in renders above. Thin 3–5 mm matte-black anodized aluminum edge around the 6 mm glass, mounted onto a recessed black birch-ply chassis inset ~25 mm from the edges.

Looks like a razor-thin ~10 mm floating designer mirror; hides the ~48 mm depth in shadow. Solid plywood back holds VESA + Pi 5 + French cleat directly.

II. Slim Dark Walnut / Black Gallery Frame

Crisp 15–18 mm mitered hardwood face (stained dark walnut to echo the beam & mahogany piano, or matte black to match your TV) with a recessed shadow reveal on the wall.

Less than half the border width of the old 38 mm pine box, with mitered corners and a rigid structural backplate.

III. Modular Aluminum Profile (Zero-Carpentry)

Custom-cut black anodized aluminum mirror extrusion assembled with internal corner brackets + black Dibond/PVC backing mask.

Dead-flat, zero wood warping, ultra-strong for the 16.2 kg glass, and assembles with just a hex key.
